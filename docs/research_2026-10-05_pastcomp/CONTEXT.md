# Our competition (context for reviewers)
PRC Data Challenge 2026 (EUROCONTROL PRU + OpenSky). Predict departure taxi-out seconds y = takeoff(MVT) − off-block(BLOCK) for 344,841 departures in Jan+Jul 2026 at 10 EU airports (EDDF EDDM EGLL EHAM LEBL LEMD LFPG LIRF LSZH LTFM). Train on 2025 (2.09M departures, labelled). Metric RMSE on a hidden set; public LB. Deadline 2026-10-11. Team "resilient-hedgehog": 38th/230, RMSE 250.9; leader 213.9.
Inputs per flight: takeoff time & runway, stand, aircraft type, schedule time (SCHED), Network Manager (NM) times (AOBT_3 = NM off-block, EOBT etc., available for ~98.5% "matched" rows), METAR, OSM stand coordinates, ADS-B (adsb.lol, partial ground coverage) for 2025 and 2026.
Current model: GBDT (LightGBM x2, CatBoost) on target residual y − (MVT − AOBT_3) for matched rows; separate model for unmatched rows; learned ADS-B residual correction; blending. NN / linear tried: no help.
Known error structure: heavy tail. RMSE dominated by rare huge errors: (a) day-rollover records (off-block stamped on wrong date, y ≈ +24h) — unpredictable; (b) airport BLOCK copied from schedule (LIRF ~8%); (c) unmatched rows (1.5% rows, 24–39% of MSE); (d) new stands in 2026 (EDDF Terminal 3, 12% of EDDF July) unseen in training; (e) CV gains sometimes don't transfer to LB (feature L5: CV −0.32 s, LB +0.12 s).
Validation folds: jan, jul (2025 months held out), jul_fwd, forward. Pre-registration + paired CI decide adoption.
RULES (must respect): no leaderboard probing / inferring labels from LB; external data only if open & documented; do not exploit side channels of blanked columns (ID ordering, second jitter); code will be GPLv3, don't copy others' code.
